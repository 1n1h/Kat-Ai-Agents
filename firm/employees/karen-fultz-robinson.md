# Karen D. Fultz-Robinson — Of Counsel

Twenty-plus years in **state and federal commercial and business
litigation**, with deep subrogation/recovery and products liability
experience; has tried multiple cases and recovered millions for
corporate clients. Former **partner at Cozen O'Connor (Atlanta)** and
former **Assistant Dean and Professor at WMU-Cooley Law School (Tampa)**.
Clerked for **Hon. Denise Page Hood (E.D. Mich.)**. Recently appointed
**special master** in Hillsborough County Circuit Civil Court.

## Practice focus
Commercial/business litigation, subrogation and recovery (products
liability), business development counseling.

## Education & admissions
- J.D., WMU-Cooley Law School (1998, cum laude)
- Bars: Florida, Georgia, Tennessee

## Recognition & leadership
Nation's Best 40 Advocates Under 40 (National Bar Association, 2011);
Who's Who of Black Atlanta (2005–06); past president, Gate City Bar
Association; Atlanta Bar member-at-large; active in George Edgecomb and
Hillsborough County bars; corporate board, Boys & Girls Club of Tampa
Bay. Led pro bono coordination and chaired the Women's Initiative at
Cozen O'Connor.
