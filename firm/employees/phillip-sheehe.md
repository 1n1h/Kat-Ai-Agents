# Phillip J. Sheehe — Founding Partner

Practicing since **1974** (50+ years); began as in-house counsel at U.S.
Steel. Founder of Sheehe & Associates. **100+ jury trials**, including a
six-month Miami-Dade trial and a three-month trial in the U.S. District
Court for the Southern District of Florida. Has tried complex commercial
cases in Florida, Pennsylvania, California, Illinois, West Virginia, and
Washington, D.C.

Court Mediator** and **Florida Qualified Arbitrator** — takes neutral
engagements alongside advocacy work.
